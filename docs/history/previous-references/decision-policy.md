# Decision policy: choose the next action, not the fanciest method

## Route

For unresolved mathematics, first load [mathematical discovery](mathematical-discovery.md).
Keep the original quantified objective immutable. These routes choose research
actions, not conjecture-resolution criteria. Persist intermediate results and
return to discovery/generalization/proof. Only a verified proof or refutation of
the original statement resolves it; budget exhaustion is suspension.

1. **Known route?** Apply and independently verify. Do not run research theater.
2. **Existing solution?** Inspect version, license, constraints, integration gap, and reproducible evidence. Adopt or wrap if sufficient.
3. **Unknown explanation?** Run a discriminating diagnostic probe.
4. **Unknown feasibility or performance?** Predeclare a baseline and the smallest meaningful PoC.
5. **Several noisy candidates?** Use a matched fixed-budget comparison; consider sequential elimination only when warranted.
6. **Strategic opponents or players?** Add game-theoretic counterplay and game-design evaluation.
7. **No reliable answer oracle?** Consider metamorphic relations, invariants, and independent cross-checks.
8. **No decision-changing test left?** Stop, preserve uncertainty, and state the blocker or next external dependency.

## Experiment selection

Prefer an action whose plausible outcomes lead to different decisions. Evaluate qualitative expected decision impact, cost, risk, reversibility, and measurement quality. Numerical expected-value or information-gain calculations are optional and require defensible estimates. Do not assign made-up probabilities or rankings.

## Stop and verdict

SOLVED: user outcome verified. SUPPORTED: scoped claim supported under conditions. REFUTED: specified falsifiable claim contradicted by valid evidence. INCONCLUSIVE: evidence insufficient or measurement invalid. BLOCKED: missing authority/capability/dependency. Preserve distinction between an invalid test and a negative result.
