## Game-theoretic and design contribution

The protocol separates proposer, falsifier and fixed verifier; accepts legal
symbolic moves; checks removed hypotheses; preserves unsuccessful candidates;
and prevents the proposer from changing the global resolution predicate. Branch
selection preserves diversity rather than rewarding example counts. The MDA
adaptation separates legal mechanics, observed research-loop dynamics and human
inspectability. These are concrete design choices informed by the sources in
the audit, not a proved equilibrium, learned self-play system or guarantee of
mathematical invention.
